# Privacy Policy for YouTube Break Reminder

Last updated: 2026-08-25

## 1. Overview
YouTube Break Reminder is designed with privacy as a top priority. All extension operations and data storage are performed strictly within the user's local browser environment (`chrome.storage.local`).

## 2. What Data We Collect
- **User Settings**: Custom time limits (by day of week/holiday), continuous break interval durations, and daily reset hour preferences.
- **Usage Activity**: Aggregated watch durations (in seconds) and 24-hour time slots on YouTube (`*.youtube.com`).

No personal identification information, browsing history outside YouTube, Google account credentials, or video titles/contents are collected.

## 3. How Data Is Stored and Transmitted
- **Local Storage Only**: All settings and usage histories are stored solely in the local browser via `chrome.storage.local`.
- **No Remote Transmission**: No data is transmitted off the user's device. We do not operate external analytics, tracking servers, or telemetry.

## 4. Third-Party Services
This extension does not use any third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics services.

## 5. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties.

## 6. Data Retention and User Control
- Daily usage history is automatically retained for up to 365 days locally and pruned thereafter.
- Users can delete all historical tracking data at any time by clicking the "履歴データを全消去" (Clear History) button in the extension's popup report tab.
- Uninstalling the extension permanently removes all stored data from the device.
